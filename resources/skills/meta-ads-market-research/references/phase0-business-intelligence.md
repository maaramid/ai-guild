# MMI Phase 0 - Business Intelligence (Internal Intelligence Database)

Purpose: capture what the business knows and believes. Everything in Section 3 is a **hypothesis** that
Step 2 (Customer Language) will check against real customer words.

## How to ask (one message, all at once)

- Send the **whole Phase 0 form below in ONE message** - all 5 parts together, as a copy-paste block the user
  can fill and send back in one go. Don't ask section by section.
- Tell them: "Sagutan mo lang lahat sa isang reply. Okay lang kung maikli, at laktawan ang hindi mo alam -
  pero sagutan ang may ★."
- When the answers come back: map them into the Template below, then show a short summary table. If any ★
  item is missing or unclear, ask for **all** the missing ones together in one short follow-up (never one
  by one). Non-★ blanks are listed as gaps - don't chase them.
- Then go straight to Step 2 (ask for the customer language).

### The form to send (copy exactly, in the user's language)

```
📋 MMI PHASE 0 - BUSINESS INFO
(Sagutan sa isang reply. ★ = kailangan. Okay lang ang maikling sagot.)

1) TUNGKOL SA BRAND
★ Ano ang business mo? (ano ang binebenta, para kanino - isang pangungusap)
- Paano mo gustong tumunog ang brand? (hal. friendly ate/kuya, expert, playful, premium)
- Mga salita o tawag mo sa customers, signature phrase o emoji, brand colors:

2) BAKIT IKAW
★ Bakit sa'yo bibili, hindi sa katabi mong seller?
★ Ano ang mahirap gayahin ng competitors sa'yo?
- Top 3-5 na magaling kayo:
- Sino-sino ang competitors mo?

3) SINO ANG CUSTOMER MO (sa tingin mo)
★ Sino ang ideal customer mo? (edad, trabaho, lugar, sitwasyon)
- Ano ang problema nila?
- Ano ang gusto nilang mangyari?
- Ano ang ayaw o kinaiinisan nila?
- Saan sila nagtitiwala? (influencer, kaibigan, reviews, groups...)

4) ANG OFFER
★ Ano ang main offer? (ano mismo ang makukuha, magkano)
- Bundles, freebies o bonus:
- Payment at delivery (COD, GCash, installment, shipping fee, free shipping):
- May darating bang promo? (payday, 11.11, anniversary...)

5) PAANO KAYO NAGBEBENTA
★ Ano ang karaniwang sinasabi ng tao bago hindi tumuloy? (objections, sa mismong salita nila)
★ Step-by-step: mula makita ang ad hanggang magbayad at matanggap
- Saan sila bumibili? (website, Messenger, Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop, live selling, store)
- Sino sumasagot sa inbox, gaano kabilis, at ano ang pinaka-madalas itanong?
- Paano niyo binibilang ang benta (lalo na sa inbox/offline)?
```

## Template

**Client/Brand Name:** · **Date Completed:** · **Answered by:**

### Section 1: Brand Foundation
- ★ **What the business does** (one sentence: what, for whom)
- **Brand Mission:** what the brand is trying to do for its customers
- **Brand Values:** 3-5
- **Brand Personality:** 3-5 traits
- **Tone of Voice:** how the brand sounds (e.g. friendly ate/kuya, expert, playful, premium)
- **Brand Characteristics:** visual/verbal identifiers - colors, recurring phrases, term of address for
  customers, signature emoji, recurring themes

### Section 2: Business Advantage
- **Top 5 Things We Do Well**
- ★ **Special Sauce:** what's hard for competitors to copy
- ★ **USP:** one sentence. Test: "Bakit sa'yo bibili, hindi sa katabi mong seller?"
- **Competitive Advantage:** why customers should choose this over alternatives
- **Known competitors:** who customers compare them with

### Section 3: Internal Customer Hypothesis
*(Business assumption only - Step 2 checks this against the customer language.)*
- ★ **Ideal Customer:**
- **Additional Traits:**
- **Problems (assumed):**
- **Wants (assumed):**
- **Hates (assumed):**
- **Trusted Sources (assumed):**
- **Dreams (assumed):**
- **Challenges (assumed):**
- **Values (assumed):**

### Section 4: Offer Intelligence
- ★ **Main Offer:** itemized - what exactly they get, at what price
- **Why It Works:** the business's reasoning
- **Bundle Opportunities:**
- **Bonuses:** current or potential, with perceived value ("worth ₱X")
- **Future Offers:**
- **Buying conditions:** payment (COD, GCash, installment), delivery, free-shipping thresholds, planned
  promos (payday, double-date sales, anniversaries, holidays)

### Section 5: Sales Intelligence
- **Qualification Questions:** what the sales process asks to know if someone's a fit
- ★ **Common Objections:** what prospects say when hesitating, in their words
- ★ **Sales Process:** step by step, from seeing the ad to paying to receiving/onboarding
- **Where they buy:** website, Messenger, Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop, live selling, walk-in
- **Inbox reality:** who replies, how fast, most-asked questions
- **How sales are counted** (especially inbox/offline sales) - so ads can later be judged on real sales
