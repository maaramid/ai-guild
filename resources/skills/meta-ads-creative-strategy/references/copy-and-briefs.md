# Copy, briefs & production

## The three jobs of an ad - Attention → Comprehension → Action (check every creative)

1. **Mapansin (Attention / thumb-stop)** - stops the scroll. People stop for *relevance*, not shock: curiosity with a connection.
   Start from one situation: "Hinahanap ng customer ang produkto ko kapag…". The first frame is the door
   (the "pinto" - the thumb-stop moment) - it deserves more effort than the caption.
2. **Maintindihan** - one main message per ad. Picture and headline point at the same situation. Only the
   key detail on screen; the rest goes in the caption.
3. **Malinaw na next step** - the CTA says exactly what to do (and what info to send in Messenger).

**Stranger test (comprehension / 5-second test):** show the draft to someone who didn't make it. Can they say what's sold, for what
situation, and how to order? If not, fix it before launch.

## Closing structure

Problem / use case → Product → Demonstration → Proof → Reason to buy → CTA.
Anti-pattern: Feature → Feature → Feature. Start from the customer's situation; show the features that
produce the outcome.

Beat sequences that work: UGC confession → product reveal → stress test → proof demo → close-up → CTA ·
"Hacks vs product" · Result first → 3 quick steps → offer · Founder: why I made it → what's different →
invitation · Testimonial: doubt → what changed → recommendation → offer card.

## Hook patterns (always tied to the situation)

Call-out ("Para sa mga may oily skin...") · Pain question ("Pagod ka na ba sa ...? 👀") · Specific
scenario ("8 hours sa work, tapos review pa pag-uwi?") · Inner monologue / quoted dialogue ("'Next month na
lang.'") · Result first · Objection flip ("Akala ko scam din 'to...") · Demonstration · Testimonial stack ·
Borrowed interest (attach the brand to a belief or picture already in their head) · Pattern break - only when it connects.

More patterns: time-bound result ("in 30 days" - only if provable) · borrowed authority/proof (reviews,
sold counts, credible experts) · comparison against the old way · new mechanism ("hindi ito X, ito ay Y").

**Story vs direct:** story-led ads suit Cold markets (more watch time, less ad resistance); people already
Deciding get short, direct, offer-led ads.

A dependable hook formula: **who it's for + the benefit + a reason to keep watching** ("Para sa mga nanay na
walang time magluto - ito yung 10-minute ulam na hindi lasang instant"). Listicle hooks work best with 3-5
items - enough to promise value, few enough to finish.

## Copy deliverable format

**Static - 3 labeled variants**, each with its own headline:
- **Direct Offer** - offer, price/value, ✅ benefit stack, 👉 CTA
- **Pain Point** - situation → empathy line → solution → proof → CTA
- **Short Form** - 1-3 lines + ✅ checklist + CTA
(Alternatives when they fit: Identity variant, Brand Intro variant.)
Plus a **bank of 5 headlines** per creative.

**Video - 2 options:**
- **Option 1** - statement-led (more English, direct)
- **Option 2** - Taglish question or quote hook

**Copy fields:** Primary text · Headline (≤ 40 chars) · Description (optional) · CTA button (Shop now /
Send message / Learn more / Book now / Get offer).

## Taglish copy conventions

- Open with a Taglish question ending in 👀, or a short ALL-CAPS statement + the brand's signature emoji.
- Quoted inner-monologue or dialogue hooks ("'Paki-explain ulit po?' 😭").
- Specific scenario openers beat generic claims.
- One empathy line ("Gets namin." / "We know the struggle.").
- Parallel contrast closers ("Hindi basta aral nang aral. Aral nang may direksyon.").
- Permission lines ("Hindi mo kailangang magaling agad.").
- ✅ value stacks, 👉 before the CTA, one consistent signature emoji per brand, the brand's term of address,
  a signature sign-off if the brand has one.
- Messenger CTAs ask for specific info ("Send us your size + location 👉").
- Testimonial formula: the doubt question → "[Name] had the same doubt at [age/situation]" → outcome today →
  how the brand helped → "let's talk" → CTA. Structure testimonials as Before → Build → After.
- PH trust signals where relevant: COD, GCash, free shipping, sold counts, real reviews, "legit".
- Reframes for price: price per day (annual ÷ 365 → "less than ₱6 a day"), annual vs monthly, "What ₱X gets
  you" stack.

## Review & approval

Status flow: Draft → Final Draft → Internal Revision → Internally Approved → Client View → Client Revision →
Final Approved. Ask the advertiser to review within 24 hours; remove old versions from the client folder.

## Monthly production rhythm (agency default - adjust per client)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Early month | Pass approved assets, launch |
| ~Day 11 | Internal creative direction for next month |
| ~Day 14 | Creative pitch to client |
| ~Day 21 | Shoot / produce next month's creatives |
| ~Day 25 | Creatives to internal approval |
| ~Day 28 | Budget proposal for next month |
| Day 29-30 | Results report - internal, then client |

## Using inbox and comments

Recurring inbox/comment questions are concepts waiting to be tested - an objection answered in an ad saves a
reply. Note where conversations stop (price, delivery fee, unanswered questions) and feed it back into
Phase 1 tagging and the offer.
