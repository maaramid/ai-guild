# MMI Phase 3 - Message Architecture & Creative Deployment

Role: senior creative strategist. Build the Validated Customer Profile, Positioning Statement, and
Messaging Pillars (Phase 2) into a deployable messaging and creative system.

Inputs: Validated Customer Profile(s) · Positioning Statement · Messaging Pillars · claim rules · External
Intelligence Database (VoC language) · gap-report angles (unvalidated) · channel/format (default Meta ads).

## Section 1: Market Temperature Read

Classify where the validated customer sits - a market-grounded read from External Intelligence, not a
generic funnel label.

| Signal from External Intelligence (quote + source) | What It Tells Us | Temperature |
|---|---|---|

Temperature scale (MMI's version of audience temperature / awareness stages):
- **Cold** - doesn't know they have the problem the way the brand frames it
- **Searching** - knows the problem, comparing solutions/categories
- **Comparing** - knows solutions exist, comparing specific brands
- **Deciding** - knows this brand, weighing objections
- **Returning** - already a customer, deciding to repeat/expand

Cite evidence for each level present. Mark a level **"not evidenced"** rather than inventing it.

## Section 2: Claim Saturation Check

- List specific claims/promises found in competitor ads, landing pages, reviews (Phase 1 competitor log +
  gap report).
- Rate overall **Claim Saturation** (MMI's competitor-claim audit of market sophistication): Low / Medium / High / Maxed Out.
- **Saturated Claims to Avoid** - so overused they read as noise or trigger skepticism.
- **Open Claim Space** - what hasn't been said that the Validated Profile would find credible (tied to
  Business Advantage).
- Mismatch risks to note: leading with a feature everyone claims, overclaiming, feature-dumping in the
  first 3 seconds. In high saturation, the usual way through is "specific problem + believable mechanism +
  visual proof", or identity/brand meaning.

## Section 3: Messaging Pillar → Proof Pairing (proof points / reasons to believe)

| Messaging Pillar | Supporting Proof (data point, stat, sales intelligence, or VoC quote - sourced) | Risk if Proof Is Missing |
|---|---|---|

No real proof yet → flag explicitly and state what to gather (testimonial, case study, demo, stat) before
this pillar can be used. Gap-report angles must pass this table to become usable.

## Section 4: Message-to-Temperature Map

| Temperature | Pillar to Lead With | Message Angle | What to Avoid Saying |
|---|---|---|---|

Guidance: Cold → the situation/problem or aspiration ("Uy, problema ko 'to"), brand and price come late;
Searching → why this approach/category; Comparing → why this brand (mechanism, comparison, objections);
Deciding → proof, guarantees, offer, payment/delivery ease; Returning → repeat, bundle, upgrade. Don't make
every ad about price - it trains the market to see a commodity.

## Section 5: Creative Concepts

3-5 concepts (not generic angles). For each:
- **Concept Name**
- **Built From:** Pillar + Temperature + Proof
- **Core Message:** one sentence
- **Concept idea:** the scenario or visual metaphor that carries it
- **Opening Lines (3-5):** in the customer's actual words - sourced from Phase 1 quotes wherever possible
- **Best Format:** matched to temperature (Cold = pattern-interrupt/relevance-first video; Deciding =
  proof-heavy static/carousel)
- **Objection Handled:** which objection (Sales Intelligence or external) it neutralizes
- **Brand reveal line** and **CTA**
- **Rationale:** why it should work for this customer

Master table:

| Concept | Pillar | Temperature | Opening Line | Format | Objection Handled | Proof status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Section 6: Deployment Sequence

- **Phase A (test first):** concepts for the temperature level with the most evidence/volume in External
  Intelligence.
- **Phase B (test second):** concepts for thinner-evidence levels - flagged higher-risk/lower-confidence.
- **Gap to monitor:** temperature levels with no concept because no pillar fit - a strategic blind spot.

Separate creative sets (usually separate campaigns) for prospecting (Cold/Searching) and retargeting
(Comparing/Deciding/Returning).

## Final output: Message Architecture Brief

All six sections in full, then 5 bullets:
1. Market Temperature this brand mostly deals with now (per evidence)
2. Claim Saturation level and the open claim space to exploit
3. The single strongest Creative Concept and why
4. The pillar with the weakest proof - what to fix before scaling spend on it
5. Recommended Phase A test lineup

Tables throughout. Every concept traces to a pillar, a temperature, and a proof point or "proof needed".
